# Physical-view artwork attribution

The Arduino Due and Raspberry Pi 4B graphics in sheet 04 are adapted from the Fritzing parts library, bundled with Fritzing 1.0.8 (CD-2843).

- Arduino Due artwork: `Arduino_DUE_V02b_breadboard.svg`, part author althaus; original title Arduino Due (Rev2b).
- Raspberry Pi 4B artwork: `raspberry-pi-4B_1_breadboard.svg`, credited in the original part to Anudit Nagar, vanepp and arnab.blue.
- Original library: https://github.com/fritzing/fritzing-parts

This physical-view image was created with Fritzing artwork. The adapted artwork and sheet 04 diagram are provided under the same CC BY-SA 3.0 license. Modifications: scaled and embedded artwork, added harness connector locations, physical wiring and labels, and illustrated additional components.

The Nexys Video illustration is a simplified original vector drawing informed by Digilent's reference layout. Its component details and the JA mating-face inset are illustrative, not a mechanical model. The interface assembly, trackball, buttons, joystick, speakers, switch and monitor are also original illustrations. The Pi model is not specified by the owner; Pi 4B artwork does not assert the actual installed Pi model.

Stock 3.3 V Due header pin location is derived from `connector253pin`; the original part labels this connector IOREF in its XML, but its board artwork places it at the 3V3 header position. The new part uses the corrected 3V3 name. Ground connector locations follow the stock artwork and share an internal ground bus.
